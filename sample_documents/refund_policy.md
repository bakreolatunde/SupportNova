# POL-BILLING-001 — Refund Policy (v1.0)

Effective: 2026-01-01

1. Duplicate charges are refunded in full within 5-7 business days once verified against the payment processor's transaction log.
2. Refunds are never promised on an exact calendar date before verification is complete — communicate "5-7 business days from verification" only.
3. Refund delays beyond 10 business days must be escalated to the Billing team lead.
